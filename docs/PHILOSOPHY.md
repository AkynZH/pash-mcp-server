# Our Philosophy: Value > Profit

At Felix, we believe that true innovation aligns the incentives of the provider and the client. Traditional SaaS models charge flat fees regardless of the value delivered, or worse, penalize efficiency. 

We chose a different path: **Fair Billing based on Real Savings**.

## Core Principles

1. **You Only Pay When You Save**
   We do not charge flat subscriptions or hidden per-request fees. Our revenue is strictly tied to the tangible value we create for you. If our PASH compression doesn't save you tokens, it doesn't earn us a dime.

2. **Strict 10% Commission on Real Savings**
   Our commission is hardcoded to exactly 10% of the *actual money saved* through token reduction. 
   - Formula: `Commission = (Raw Tokens - Compressed Tokens) * Token Price * 0.10`
   - No hidden markups. No "platform fees". No "additional surcharges".

3. **Radical Transparency**
   Every month, you receive a cryptographic-grade telemetry report detailing:
   - Exact raw token count.
   - Exact compressed token count.
   - The precise dollar amount saved.
   - Our 10% commission, calculated openly and verifiably.

4. **Engineered for Efficiency**
   Because our revenue depends on your savings, we are obsessively motivated to make our compression algorithms faster, smarter, and more aggressive. Every byte we save you is a byte that benefits us both.

## The Promise
We are not here to extract rent. We are here to eliminate waste. 
By aligning our success with your efficiency, we build a partnership rooted in trust, transparency, and measurable results.
